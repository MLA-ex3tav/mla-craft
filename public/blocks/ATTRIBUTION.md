Minecraft textures belong to Mojang/Microsoft. These assets are not original project artwork. This project is not an official Minecraft product and is not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.

Icons are bundled locally so no requests to Block Render are made at runtime.

## Player inventory

Inventory item images are loaded on demand from https://blockrender.dev/render/item/<vanilla-id>.png?size=64. Minecraft textures belong to Mojang/Microsoft. Modded and unavailable items use a labeled fallback.

Public player skins are displayed through https://mc-heads.net/player/<identity>. The preview represents the public skin, not equipped armor or server-side custom skins.
